Sea Serpent Sushi

Description

Sweet and splicy sea serpent meets sweet and spicy sauce.

Ingredients

  • Seasoned sushi rice, cooled to room temperature
  • Roasted nori (seaweed) sheets
  • Steamed sea serpent whiskers
  • Strips of cucumber
  • Sweet chili sauce
  • Lightly roasted sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Prep the fillings (warm the kraken)
  2. Prepare your rolling station
  3. Lay the nori and rice
  4. Flip it over
  5. Add the fillings
  6. Roll it up
  7. Slice and garnish
