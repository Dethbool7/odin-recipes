Merfolk Tail Sushi

Description

Crunchy fried merfolk tail nestled in carrot and cucumber while garnished with tempura flakes.

Ingredients

  • Seasoned sushi rice, cooled to room temperature
  • Roasted nori (seaweed) sheets
  • Fried merfolk tail
  • Strips of cucumber
  • strips of carrot
  • Lightly roasted sesame seeds
  • Tempura flakes

Instructions

  1. Prep the fillings
  2. Prepare your rolling station
  3. Lay the nori and rice
  4. Flip it over
  5. Add the fillings
  6. Roll it up
  7. Slice and garnish
